"Baka", the Baka logo and the names of Baka's paid options are marks of Zima Blue (the owner). The license grants no right to use them, except as the license itself says (section 6): describing where the code comes from.

## You may

- Say your product **works with**, **is compatible with**, or **is built on** Baka, in plain text, as long as it is true and does not suggest the owner made, checked or endorses it.
- Name Baka in documentation, articles, talks, issues and comparisons.
- Package, mirror and redistribute unmodified Baka under its own name, with its license and notices.
- Publish packs, recipes and tools whose names make clear what they are for (for example "ACME recipes for Baka"), as long as the name cannot be taken for the official project.

## You may not

- Call a fork or a modified build "Baka", or use a name or logo so close to it that people could mistake it for the official one.
- Use the name or logo of Baka or of its paid options for a hosted service, a company or a domain, or in a way that suggests a partnership, endorsement or official status you do not have.
- Sell or offer something as an official Baka paid option, or issue or imitate a license key.

A fork should pick its own name and logo. Changed builds that keep the name need the owner's written permission.
